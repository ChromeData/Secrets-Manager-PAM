WHAT RAN
  KMS customer managed key with rotation enabled, its key policy, the
  consumer and rotation IAM roles, and the secret itself. Retrieval was
  exercised end to end: the secret is encrypted with the CMK and the value
  comes back intact.

  The kms:ViaService condition is present on the deployed key policy,
  which is the control that stops the consumer role taking ciphertext
  elsewhere and unwrapping it. That one has no clean CyberArk equivalent
  and it is worth pointing at in the comparison doc.

WHAT DID NOT RUN, AND WHY
  CloudTrail is a LocalStack Pro feature. A full apply hangs on it, so the
  audit half of this lab, terraform/audit.tf, could not be exercised here.
  That is the half carrying the lab's most important finding, that reads
  are invisible until you enable data events and pay per event. It stays
  unverified until a real AWS run.

  Rotation is enabled on the secret but an actual rotation was not
  triggered: LocalStack community Lambda execution is unreliable for the
  four step protocol and a green result here would not mean much.

  LocalStack also does not evaluate resource policy at request time, so
  prove-denied is still a real AWS test.
